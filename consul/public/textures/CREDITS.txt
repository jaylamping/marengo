starmap_2020_4k.exr
-------------------
NASA Scientific Visualization Studio — "Deep Star Maps 2020" (SVS ID 4851)
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851

Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC.

Equirectangular (plate carrée) star map in celestial coordinates, 4096x2048,
OpenEXR half-float. Plotted from Hipparcos-2, Tycho-2, and Gaia DR2 catalogs
(1.7 billion stars). Used as the Consul scene background.
