# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web — latest Chrome only (personal cockpit; no legacy browser matrix). React Compiler is enabled in Vite.

## Users

Sole operator: the builder of Marengo. Context is long bench and lab sessions — observing live state, training behaviors, tuning control, and commanding the physical humanoid without friction or ceremony.

## Product Purpose

Consul is the personal operator cockpit for Marengo: observe, train, and control everything about the robot from one web surface. Success is knowing machine health and posture in seconds, changing a gain or command as one continuous thought with the 3D view and telemetry, and never losing control when something goes wrong at 2 a.m.

## Positioning

The most performant and functional robotics operator app that still feels beautiful — through transitions and micro-interactions that are felt, then forgotten; once noticed, they are already in the way.

## Brand Personality

**Precise · Calm · Armed.** Voice is direct and technical. Tone stays quiet under load: color and motion mean state, not decoration. Emotional goal is certainty and control of expensive hardware.

## Anti-references

Standard shadcn / flavor-of-the-month dashboard kits — same structural patterns may appear, but never as stock chrome. No element anywhere that does not serve a purpose.

## Design Principles

1. **Function is the aesthetic** — every pixel must make the operator faster, more certain, or more in control of the physical machine.
2. **Felt, not noticed** — motion and polish earn their place only while they stay subliminal; if you see the effect, it is already noise.
3. **No empty structure** — shared UI patterns are fine; purposeless cards, badges, and kit leftovers are not.
4. **Armed calm** — scarcity of chroma and chrome so status changes read as signal, not theme.
5. **One continuous thought** — tuning, viewing, and commanding should feel like a single gesture across surfaces, not a tour of panels.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Personal single-operator tool: no WCAG target. Prefer reduced-motion and legible status contrast when cheap; do not block shipping on a11y compliance.
