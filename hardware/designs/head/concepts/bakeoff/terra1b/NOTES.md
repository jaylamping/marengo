# marengo head — targeted polish

The three master images were not present in the packet. Likeness judgments therefore use the ratios recorded in `packet/fable_r3/NOTES.md`, not a new measurement of the film images. The approved raised head and ringed neck are retained.

1. **Crest:** Used straight-sided loft sections and included the peak and the end of its descending edge as explicit sections. This removes the small secondary spike behind the main peak while retaining the thin, swept blade. Serves the recorded MASTER_B/C crest silhouette. The side preview shows one peak.
2. **Jaw plate knee:** Moved the front of the side plate from x = -26 to -6.5 mm. From the nominal x = 65 mm face this places the knee 71.5 mm back, or 0.55 of the 130 mm head depth, matching the recorded MASTER_C barn view. The pivot and rounded chin remain in place.
3. **Eye openings:** Increased the glowing disc diameter from 28 to 30 mm, retaining the 36 mm outer ring and eye spacing. This satisfies the original display-opening requirement while keeping the inquisitive round eyes and the recorded MASTER_A ring and spacing proportions. The resulting glow is 0.24 of the nominal 124 mm width versus the recorded master ratio of 0.21; this is a practical constraint, not a likeness improvement.

The jaw band was left at its existing section width: the recorded front view gives a band projection of about 0.06 head widths per side, versus 0.056 in round 3. The maximum jaw/plate bounding width after the knee change is approximately 142 mm, outside the brief's approximate 125–135 mm head-width target; narrowing it would compromise the documented reference match. The recorded references should be rechecked directly if available. No other taste-driven changes were applied.

Export check: OpenCascade reported one face skipped during triangulation. Trimesh reports the exported head, crest, glow and jaw meshes are not watertight, although the CAD solids report valid. The exports need mesh repair and verification before fabrication.
