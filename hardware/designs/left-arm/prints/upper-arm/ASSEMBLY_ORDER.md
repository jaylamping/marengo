# Left upper arm assembly order

Torque values are not set, they come from the dummy output test. Hand tight with a short driver until then. All sleeve ends must be square.

1. **Prepare the prints.** Clear support as in `PRINT_ORDER.md`. Sand the hoop inboard face flat. Sand or file the ring notch clean if you notched it by hand.
2. **Sleeves.** Fit the six hoop sleeves and six hub sleeves. Check each sleeve end against the steel faces it will clamp.
3. **Bearing.** Press the 6808 into the carrier seat. Fit the three retainer screws and washers so they clamp the outer race.
4. **Elbow RS02.** Slide it into the Ø66 bore until it stops on the seat at X 165. Fit the elbow plug through the port slot now, because the room is limited later.
5. **Ribbon, fixed end.** Bring the 17.8 mm ribbon from the tube cavity down the port slot on the carrier side, X 152 to 156, clear of the plug. Pass it through the steel ring notch and lay it flat in the carrier slot, on the X 149.7 face, at least 10 mm along. Bond it there. Leave the free end long, for the clock-spring.
6. **Carrier.** Fit the steel ring and carrier together over the ribbon, with the notch at the top toward the tube and the washers in the Ø6.4 holes. Fit the six hoop screws and washers. Check the ribbon isn't pinched by hand before tightening.
7. **Yaw RS02.** Put the hub ring on the flat at Y 298. Fit the six M4 × 30 hub screws through the sleeves into the yaw output. Prove the tool access on a dummy first, the top hub is tight.
8. **Trunk and fuses.** Run the trunk through the cover entry. Make the M14 splice near the shoulder end. Fit the two inline fuse holders in the pockets on the wall opposite the window, Y 260 to 290, held with hook and loop tape so they can be lifted out to the window to change a fuse. Check each joint through its range: no pull, pinch or rub.
9. **Cover.** Fit the cover with four M3 screws once the harness checks pass.
10. **P2.** If you made P2 L05 or filed the tip, the inward roll bench limit can go from 2 to 5 degrees, once you have checked 5 degrees by hand.

The forearm and elbow rig test follow. See `../../testing/ELBOW_DUMMY_OUTPUT_TEST.md`.
