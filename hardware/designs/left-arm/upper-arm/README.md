# Left upper arm, first body

2026-11-05. First body, not reviewed and not released for printing or load. The CAD stays on Joey-PC.

The arm carries the yaw RS02 output at the top and holds the elbow RS02 housing at the bottom.

- Section: closed hollow tube, 46 mm wide and 78.5 mm deep, 3 mm walls, 8 mm top plate.
- Cables: 14 mm bore through the top plate, the tube as the channel, a rear window with a bolt-on cover, and a slot down to the RS02 port.
- Joints: M4 and M3 clearance holes sized for compression limiters. Inserts are specified, not modelled.
- Elbow support: bolted carrier with a stub for a 6808 bearing (40 × 52 × 7 mm), hollow for cables.
- Stops: elbow lugs on the carrier for 0° and 135°. They need the forearm fork to act against.

Mass is about 170 g as solid PETG for the arm, carrier and cover.

Open items:

- No strength check yet. The rear window is the weakest section.
- Shoulder roll stop is not here: it belongs on P1 and P2, at the roll joint.
- No bearing retainer or forearm fork yet.
- Small overlaps with the RS02 model at the bolt holes need checking.
