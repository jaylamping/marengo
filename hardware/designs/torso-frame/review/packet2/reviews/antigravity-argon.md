### Question 2 — Electrical-Mechanical Safety & the `1 MΩ || 10 nF` Frame Bond
1. **Where a crash, pinched wire, or loose screw can short `PACK+` ($54.6\text{ V}$), `VMOT`, or a phase wire to the frame**:
   - **Top of HP board (`Y = 340..353.6, Z = 43.5..60`)**: The unswitched `PACK+` M4 ring-lug terminal (upstream of the BMS FETs and E-stop switches, fused only at $60\text{ A}$ inside the pack with $607\text{ A}$ short-circuit current) and `L_ARM`/`R_ARM` terminals sit directly under `rail_shared_1` (`Y[353.62, 373.62], Z[50, 70]`) and beside `br2, br14`.    - **Front post inner edges (`|X| = 50..55, Z = 50..70`)**: Corner bracket screw heads `f0_1_h, f2_1_h, f12_1_h, f14_1_h` reach `|X| = 50.5 mm` — only **$0.50\text{ mm}$** from the `pdb` / `pdb_logic` PCB edge (`|X| = 50.0`), while `harness_down_±1` overlaps `rail_bot_1`, `rail_shared_1`, and `br0, br2, br12, br14` by **$-4.00\text{ mm}$** and collides with board mounting screws `pdb_m3_0..5_h` (**$-0.41\text{ mm}$**).
   - **Side exit slots (`|X|[50, 55], Z[40, 50]`)**: M4 ring lugs for `CHARGE` (tied to `PACK+`), `WAIST`, `AUX`, and `LEGS` exit through a $5\text{ mm}$ slot right against the bare corner of `post_lo_±1_1` (`|X| = 55, Z = 50`).
   - **Shoulder harness (`harness_shoulder_±1`)**: Penetrates the metal `jetson` carrier/heatsink envelope by **$-5.00\text{ mm}$**.
2. **Does the single-point `1 MΩ || 10 nF` frame bond ([`data/README.md`](file:///Users/joseph/scratch/frame-gate2/hardware/designs/torso-frame/review/packet2/data/README.md#L207)) make this worse? Yes, in both possible actuator-isolation cases**:
   - **Case A (Frame is otherwise DC-isolated from `GND`)**: If `PACK+` ($54.6\text{ V}$) shorts to the aluminium frame, current through the $1\text{ M}\Omega$ resistor to `GND` is $54.6\text{ }\mu\text{A}$. Neither the $60\text{ A}$ fuse nor the BMS/INA241 trips. 2. **Can the boards be removed? Walk the steps**:

### Question 7 — Step Joint Under $+2.2\text{ kg}$ Pack Mass & Ordering Gussets Before the End Anchor
1. **Is the step joint worse with the $2.2\text{ kg}$ pack mass added low in the lower box?**
