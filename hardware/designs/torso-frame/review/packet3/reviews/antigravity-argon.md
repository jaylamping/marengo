## 2. Answers to the Eight Questions

### Q1. Tip-Over Load Case: Failure Hierarchy, $g$-Levels, and Pack Capture
Using $1\,g = 21.58\text{ N}$ for the $2.20\text{ kg}$ bare pack ($23.74\text{ N}$ for $2.42\text{ kg}$ with on-pack BMS/connectors):

1. **Upward ($+Y$, inverted / pitch-over rebound) — Top Strap Hooks fail first at $\mathbf{3.5\text{–}15\,g}$, then M4 inserts/bosses at $\mathbf{23\text{–}37\,g}$:**
   - **Strap hook cantilever bending:** `Tongue_end` ([build_cradle.py](file:///C:/Temp/marengo-packet3-argon/hardware/designs/torso-frame/review/packet3/scripts/build_cradle.py#L197)) cuts away $Z \in [-15, -7]\text{ mm}$ at the web slot ($|X| = 48.0\text{ mm}$), leaving only a $b = 12.0\text{ mm} \times h = 3.0\text{ mm}$ bar through each slot ($S = \frac{b h^2}{6} = 18.0\text{ mm}^3$, $M_y = \sigma_y S = 45 \times 18 = \mathbf{810\text{ N}\cdot\text{mm}}$ per hook).
     - Right hook overhangs $42.5\text{ mm}$ across the pack ($X \in [-47.5, -5.0]$); left hook overhangs $22.5\text{ mm}$ ($X \in [+25.0, +47.5]$).
     - For distributed/centroid contact ($e_R = 21.25\text{ mm}, e_L = 11.25\text{ mm}$), right hook yields at $F_R = \frac{810}{21.25} = \mathbf{38.1\text{ N}}$ and left at $F_L = \frac{810}{11.25} = \mathbf{72.0\text{ N}}$ ($\mathbf{76\text{–}110\text{ N} = 3.5\text{–}5.1\,g}$). Even if pack corner contact stays within $e = 5\text{–}10\text{ mm}$ of $|X| = 48.0\text{ mm}$ before hook deflection shifts contact or unclips the $1.1\text{ mm}$ barb, both hooks yield at $F = \frac{2 \times 810}{5\text{ to }10} = \mathbf{162\text{–}324\text{ N}}$ ($\mathbf{7.5\text{–}15.0\,g}$).
   - **Top foot overhang above `Strap_slot`:** The slot ceiling ($Y = 348.9\text{ mm}, |X| \in [48.0, 51.5]\text{ mm}$) pushes $+Y$ on the unchamfered $4.0\text{ mm}$ `Top_foot`, which is cantilevered $e_X = 55.0 - 49.75 = 5.25\text{ mm}$ inboard of the shelf rail edge ($|X| = 55.0\text{ mm}$). With $b_{\text{eff}} \approx 22.5\text{ mm}$ ($S = 60\text{ mm}^3$) and step notch $K_t = 1.5$ at $(|X|=51.5, Y=349.62)$, each top foot yields at $F_Y = \frac{45 \times 60}{1.5 \times 5.25} = \mathbf{343\text{ N/side}}$ ($\mathbf{686\text{ N} = 31.8\,g}$), then closes the $0.98\text{ mm}$ gap against the compute shelf (`shelf`, $Y = 354.6\text{ mm}$).
   - **Capture in $+Y$:** **Captured** by the compute shelf plate (`shelf`, $Y = 354.6\text{ mm}$) and shared rails ($Y = 353.62\text{ mm}$), **but** the $8.6\text{ mm}$ gap ($Y \in [345.0, 353.62]\text{ mm}$) contains the $5.2\text{–}5.8\text{ mm}$ OD 10 AWG main pack leads ([data/cradle/README.md](file:///C:/Temp/marengo-packet3-argon/hardware/designs/torso-frame/review/packet3/data/cradle/README.md#L59)): if the pack rises $> 2.8\text{ mm}$, it crushes the 10 AWG power leads against the shelf/shared rail (short-circuit hazard).

2. **Lateral ($\pm X$, side fall) — Web top neck & window columns fail at $\mathbf{6.6\text{–}15\,g}$:**
   - Because `Strap_slot` severs $Z \in [-50.0, +5.2]\text{ mm}$ at the top of the web, the rear $55.2\text{ mm}$ of the upper web is a free cantilever corner, and the `Front_lip` stiffener terminates at $Y = 345.0\text{ mm}$ and $Y = 179.0\text{ mm}$ ([build_cradle.py](file:///C:/Temp/marengo-packet3-argon/hardware/designs/torso-frame/review/packet3/scripts/build_cradle.py#L132)).
   - At the top root neck ($Y = 345.5\text{ mm}, Z \in [5.2, 37.5]\text{ mm}$, $b = 32.3\text{ mm}, t = 3.5\text{ mm}$), $S_{\text{neck}} = \frac{32.3 \times 3.5^2}{6} = \mathbf{65.9\text{ mm}^3}$ ($M_y = 2966\text{ N}\cdot\text{mm}$). Across the window span ($L_Y = 166.5\text{ mm}$), a central lateral load on one web gives $M \approx \frac{F_X L_Y}{8} = 20.8\,F_X$, yielding at $F_X = \mathbf{142\text{ N}}$ ($\mathbf{6.6\,g}$ on one web; $\mathbf{13.1\,g}$ across both webs).
   - For the upper-rear pack corner ($Z \in [-45, +5.2]$) pushing outward on the severed web below `Strap_slot` (bridge height above upper window $h = 345.5 - 332.0 = 13.5\text{ mm}$, $t = 3.5\text{ mm}$, $S = \frac{13.5 \times 3.5^2}{6} = 27.6\text{ mm}^3$, $M_y = 1240\text{ N}\cdot\text{mm}$, lever arm $\sim 25\text{ mm}$ to $Z = +5.2$), local fracture initiates at $\sim 50\text{ N}$ corner force ($\mathbf{9\text{–}15\,g}$ total pack load).
   - **Capture in $\pm X$:** **Captured** inside the lower box after $3.5\text{–}7.5\text{ mm}$ lateral travel by the aluminum lower posts/rails at $|X| = 55.0\text{ mm}$ (though the carrier pads and board standoffs take secondary shear).

3. **Rearward ($-Z$, back fall) — M4 pack inserts tear out / web slot root fractures at $\mathbf{21\text{–}37\,g}$ (PACK NOT CAPTURED):**
   - **There is no rear lip or rear bar**, and the D2 door is magnet-latched with a single M3 lock screw ([data/frame/README.md](file:///C:/Temp/marengo-packet3-argon/hardware/designs/torso-frame/review/packet3/data/frame/README.md#L228): *"the D2 door is not a restraint"*).
   - The **only** $-Z$ restraint is the two M4 thumbscrews at $(Y = 252.6, Z = -5.0)$ penetrating $4.0\text{–}5.0\text{ mm}$ into M4 heat-set inserts in the pack side wall.
   - Each screw acts across a moment arm $e_X \approx 4.5\text{ mm}$ (from web mid-plane $|X| = 49.75\text{ mm}$ to insert mid-depth $|X| = 45.25\text{ mm}$), applying combined shear $V$ and prying moment $M = 4.5\,V$ to a $\le 5\text{ mm}$ insert with $\le 1\text{ mm}$ backing wall before the 21700 cells. Insert tear-out / boss splitting in printed plastic occurs at $V \approx 250\text{–}400\text{ N/insert}$ ($\mathbf{500\text{–}800\text{ N total} = 23\text{–}37\,g}$ bare pack, $\mathbf{21\text{–}34\,g}$ with BMS).
   - Simultaneously, $-Z$ force at the boss ($Y = 252.6$) cannot reach the rear top M5 bolt ($Z = -30$) directly because `Strap_slot` severs $Z \in [-50, +5.2]$; in-plane tearing at the unfilleted slot root $(Z = +5.2, Y = 345.5)$ and buckling of the $9.0 \times 3.5\text{ mm}$ rear window column ($Z \in [-50, -41]$) fail at $\sim \mathbf{25\text{–}35\,g}$.
   - **Capture in $-Z$:** **NOT CAPTURED.** Once the two pack inserts tear out or the webs fracture, the $2.42\text{ kg}$ pack + BMS slides straight out $-Z$ into the non-structural D2 door, crushing the rear-mounted JK BMS ($Z = -65\text{ mm}$) first and yanking the top XT90 power leads.

4. **Downward ($-Y$, hard vertical landing) — `Bottom_foot_tongue` neck at $|X| = 55.0\text{ mm}$ fails at $\mathbf{28\text{–}42\,g}$ (4× weaker than the ledge):**
   - **Ledge (`Ledge`) itself:** $b = 79\text{ mm}, h = 4.0\text{ mm}$ ($S = 210.7\text{ mm}^3$), load centroid at $|X| = 43.75\text{ mm}$ ($e = 48.0 - 43.75 = 4.25\text{ mm}$ to web inner face). With sharp corner $K_t = 2.0$, ledge root yields at $F = \frac{45 \times 210.7}{2.0 \times 4.25} = \mathbf{1115\text{ N/side}}$ ($\mathbf{103\,g}$).
   - **Hidden weak link (`Bottom_foot_tongue` at $|X| = 55.0\text{ mm}$):** The aluminum bottom side rail only starts at $|X| = 55.0\text{ mm}$. `Bottom_foot_strip` ($|X| \in [51.5, 55.0]$, $Z \in [-44, +37.5]$) sits entirely over air, and `Bottom_root` chamfer tapers to $0\text{ mm}$ height right at $|X| = 55.0\text{ mm}$ ([build_cradle.py](file:///C:/Temp/marengo-packet3-argon/hardware/designs/torso-frame/review/packet3/scripts/build_cradle.py#L137)). Only `Bottom_foot_tongue` ($b = 43.0\text{ mm}, h = 4.0\text{ mm}, S = 114.7\text{ mm}^3$) crosses onto the rail at $|X| = 55.0\text{ mm}$, carrying the full downward load across a lever arm $e = 55.0 - 43.75 = \mathbf{11.25\text{ mm}}$!
   - Yield per side at $|X| = 55.0\text{ mm}$: $F_{-Y,\text{side}} = \frac{45 \times 114.7}{K_t \times 11.25} = \mathbf{459\text{ N}}$ ($K_t = 1.0$) to $\mathbf{306\text{ N}}$ ($K_t = 1.5$ at the sharp $Z = \pm 21.5\text{ mm}$ step), giving $\mathbf{612\text{–}918\text{ N total} = 28\text{–}42\,g}$.
   - **Capture in $-Y$:** **Captured** by the waist adapter plate ($Y = 155\text{ mm}$), **but** the Arcol HS25 brake resistor (`brake_res`, $Y_{\max} = 182.0\text{ mm}$, [parts.json](file:///C:/Temp/marengo-packet3-argon/hardware/designs/torso-frame/review/packet3/data/frame/parts.json)) sits just **$1.0\text{ mm}$ below the pack bottom ($Y = 183.0\text{ mm}$)** at $Z \in [33.4, 34.0]\text{ mm}$! Downward deflection $> 1.0\text{ mm}$ drives the pack bottom directly onto the metal brake resistor housing.

5. **Forward ($+Z$, front fall) — Foot M5 friction slips at $\mathbf{44\text{–}74\,g}$ (hits bottom brackets after $0.5\text{ mm}$), Front Lip / Web Twisting yields at $\mathbf{45\text{–}65\,g}$:**

With button heads clamping directly on slotted PETG (no washers; retained preload after PETG creep $F_p \approx 600\text{–}1000\text{ N/screw}$, $\mu \approx 0.20$), friction slips at $8 \times 0.20 \times (600\text{ to }1000) = \mathbf{960\text{–}1600\text{ N}}$ ($\mathbf{44\text{–}74\,g}$). After $0.5\text{ mm}$ slip, `Bottom_foot_tongue` ($Z = +21.5\text{ mm}$) impacts corner brackets `br1`/`br11` at $Z = +22.0\text{ mm}$, while the top foot slides up to $2.5\text{ mm}$, racking the web.
   - **Front lip (`Front_lip`):** Root bending of the lip itself ($b = 162\text{ mm}, h = 3.5\text{ mm}, S = 330.8\text{ mm}^3, e = 3.25\text{ mm}$) is strong ($\sim 2290\text{ N/side}$ at $K_t = 2$), **but** the $5.0\text{ mm}$ X-offset between the lip load ($|X| = 44.75\text{ mm}$) and the web mid-plane ($|X| = 49.75\text{ mm}$) twists the $3.5\text{ mm}$ web out-of-plane over its $166.5\text{ mm}$ vertical span, folding the lip/web inward and forward by $> 3.0\text{ mm}$ into the board carrier at $\sim \mathbf{45\text{–}65\,g}$.

### Q2. Adequacy of the 4.7 mm Web Strip Beside the Strap Slot
- **Inadequate — and the author misidentified the geometry:**
  - [PACKET.md](file:///C:/Temp/marengo-packet3-argon/hardware/designs/torso-frame/review/packet3/PACKET.md#L38) and [data/cradle/README.md](file:///C:/Temp/marengo-packet3-argon/hardware/designs/torso-frame/review/packet3/data/cradle/README.md#L34) state: *"from Z −50 to 5 only a 4.7 mm strip joins each web to its top foot"*.
  - In [`build_side`](file:///C:/Temp/marengo-packet3-argon/hardware/designs/torso-frame/review/packet3/scripts/build_cradle.py#L169), `box(47, 53, 345.5, 348.9, -51, 5.2, "Strap_slot", cut=True)` cuts **all the way through the $3.5\text{ mm}$ web** ($|X| \in [48.0, 51.5]\text{ mm}$) from $Z = -50.0\text{ mm}$ to $Z = +5.2\text{ mm}$.

### Q3. Thumbscrew Bowing ($0.5\text{ mm}$ per Side, Outward-In Pull)
- **What happens to the webs and clamp:**
  - Because the two thumbscrews pull the pack outward toward opposite webs across a $1.0\text{ mm}$ total gap ($2 \times 0.5\text{ mm}$), tightening both screws places the two webs and the two pack heat-set inserts in an **opposing tension fight**.
  - Lateral bending stiffness of one web at the boss $(Y = 252.6, Z = -5.0)$ over the $166.5\text{ mm}$ vertical span (with $64\%$ of the span cut by $62\text{ mm}$ windows and `Front_lip` ending at $Y = 179$ and $345$) is only $k_{\text{web}} \approx \mathbf{5\text{–}10\text{ N/mm}}$. Closing the $0.5\text{ mm}$ gap takes only **$2.5\text{–}5\text{ N}$** ($5\text{–}10\text{ N}$ if the pack shifts against one web and the opposite web must bow the full $1.0\text{ mm}$).
  - By contrast, even light finger torque ($T \approx 0.15\text{–}0.30\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$) on an $\varnothing 18\text{ mm}$ M4 thumbscrew generates $F = \frac{T}{0.2 d} \approx \mathbf{190\text{–}375\text{ N}}$ axial force — $40\text{–}100\times$ the force required to bow the web.
  - Consequently: (a) a "snug only" / torque limit is impossible to regulate by feel and leaves **zero preload** so the screws vibrate loose; (b) bowing pinches the pack only at mid-height ($Y = 252.6\text{ mm}$), leaving top/bottom free to rattle by $\pm 0.5\text{ mm}$ and tilting the boss face against the screw head; (c) under warm PETG creep, the webs take a permanent inward set that binds future pack swaps; and (d) $1.0\text{ mm}$ inward bow on one side pushes the $16\text{ mm}$ screw tip to $5.5\text{ mm}$ inside the pack case ($0.5\text{ mm}$ from the $6.0\text{ mm}$ cell-puncture limit).
- **Fix (Redesign datum + spacer/shoulder, not torque limit):**
  - Do **not** pull from both sides across an air gap. Make **one** web inner face (or two full-height $0.4\text{ mm}$ vertical guide ribs at $Z = -35$ and $+25\text{ mm}$ on one web) the fixed lateral datum, and on the **other** web add a local $0.5\text{ mm}$ inboard boss pad (ramped $1\text{ mm}$ at its $-Z$ edge for slide-in) OR use a shoulder screw that bottoms on the brass insert face so tightening clamps solid metal-to-metal without bending the web.

### Q6. Thermal Risk at 36 W Pack Heat in a Closed Shell
- **High risk of PETG creep, preload loss, and heat trapping:**
  1. **Total lower-box heat is $\sim 50\text{ W+}$, not $36\text{ W}$, with no exhaust:** Alongside the $36\text{ W}$ pack ($20\text{ A}$ discharge), the lower box contains the on-pack JK BMS ($\sim 3\text{–}5\text{ W}$), the power/logic board (`pdb`, $\ge 10\text{ W}$ from DC-DC and E-stop FETs), and the Arcol HS25 brake resistor (`brake_res`, $1.0\text{ mm}$ below the pack bottom and $3.0\text{ mm}$ from the PETG ledges), while [data/frame/README.md](file:///C:/Temp/marengo-packet3-argon/hardware/designs/torso-frame/review/packet3/data/frame/README.md#L230) notes *"the lower box has no exhaust"*.
  2. **The $3\text{ mm}$ carrier-to-pack air gap is laterally sealed into a dead-air chimney:** The cradle's `Front_lip` ($|X| \in [42, 48]\text{ mm}, Y \in [179, 345]\text{ mm}, Z \in [34.0, 37.5]\text{ mm}$) completely blocks both vertical sides of the $3.5\text{ mm}$ gap between the pack ($Z = 34.0$) and the carrier ($Z = 37.5$). Front intake fans blow onto the front of the solid $100 \times 150\text{ mm}$ PCB ($Z = 47.5$), which blocks airflow through the carrier windows.
  3. **Temperature rise & PETG softening ($T_g \approx 78\,^\circ\text{C}$, HDT $\approx 65\,^\circ\text{C}$, creep onset $\approx 50\text{–}55\,^\circ\text{C}$):**
     - Across the $0.5\text{ mm}$ air gap ($U = k_{\text{air}} / \delta = 0.027 / 0.0005 = 54\text{ W/(m}^2\cdot\text{K)}$), the PETG web inner face tracks within $2\text{–}3\,\text{K}$ of the pack surface.
     - Over a single $20\text{ A}$ discharge ($864\text{ s}$, $Q = 31.1\text{ kJ}$, pack $C_{\text{th}} \approx 1980\text{ J/K}$), adiabatic pack rise is $15.7\,\text{K}$ ($\sim 11\,\text{K}$ net), and in a closed shell with $50\text{ W}$ total dissipation and warm ambient/recharge conditions, local pack/air temperatures reach **$55\text{–}70\,^\circ\text{C}$** (and $> 100\,^\circ\text{C}$ on the HS25 brake resistor $3\text{ mm}$ from the bottom ledge).
     - At $55\text{–}65\,^\circ\text{C}$, PETG under continuous stress (un-washered M5 button heads on slotted feet, bowed webs, heat-set inserts, and flexed snap tongues) creeps rapidly, losing bolt preload and permanently deforming.
