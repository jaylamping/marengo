## Bench-behaviour changes

- A stalled move toward a **negative** target now trips `AscentStall` after 2 s (previously unfused). A positive-direction move toward a negative target from below it is no longer watched.
- Retargets no longer grant a fresh fuse budget. A stalled or sagging joint that is retargeted keeps its accrued stall time.
- `wave` is refused (not clamped) when its range leaves the soft limits, when its peak speed or acceleration exceeds the joint's velocity cap, `position_trajectory_velocity_rad_s` or `position_trajectory_accel_rad_s2`, or when any argument is non-finite. A jammed wave joint trips `WaveStall` after 2 s of commanded motion.
- `hold-at NaN/inf` is refused with `NonFiniteTarget` instead of being latched.
- MissingSetpoint/Dynamics/GainShape tick errors now latch a Controller fault (cleared through the normal fault-clear path).
- No change to MIT wire output at steady state; the wire kp was already the ramped kp.
